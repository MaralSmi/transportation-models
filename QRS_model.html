QRS Model Calculator (Quick Response System)

Urban travel impedance evaluation and mode share estimation model based on travel time, excess time, and out-of-pocket costs.

Iijm = ttravel + 2.5·texcess + 3·(Cost / Incomemin)
MSa = Itb / (Itb + Iab)
This tool performs all calculations related to travel impedance ($I_{ijm}$) and trip distribution.
Important Note Implemented in Code: According to the model rules, parking cost is automatically divided by 2 in the formulas (since we calculate one-way trips). Additionally, travel cost is considered as (cost per km × distance).
